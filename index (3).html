Forecast is an experimental, weather-unadjusted trend projection combining 24-hour (50%), 48-hour (30%), and approximately seven-day (20%) flow changes, when available. The trend gradually tapers toward zero; conflicting recent and weekly trends reduce its strength. The shaded uncertainty range reflects a heuristic volatility allowance, not a calibrated statistical prediction interval. Storms, snowmelt, diversions and releases can invalidate the projection. No official USGS forecast is implied.
